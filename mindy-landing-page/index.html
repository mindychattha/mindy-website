Do I need to be pre-approved before this call?

No. Most buyers book this call specifically to understand that process first.

What if I'm not ready to buy for another 6–12 months?

Still worth booking. Knowing your timeline early means you can plan around it instead of scrambling later.

Does the mortgage stress test affect me?

Likely, yes — it applies to most Canadian buyers regardless of lender. We'll cover exactly what it means for your specific numbers on the call.
